## Observation Session 1: Central Library Ground Floor & 2nd Floor Reading Room
- **Date & Time:** September 15, 2026 | 12:45 PM – 1:45 PM (Peak lunch break / shift transition)
- **Observer:** Research Team Member (Silent Observation)

| Dimension | Real Observations Noted in Field |
|---|---|
| **A (Activities)** | Students rapidly packing laptops and backpacks to catch lunch; students returning to desks searching frantically under chairs; students approaching the front circulation counter asking verbal questions. |

## Observation Session 2: Block 34 Main Entrance Security Post
- **Date & Time:** September 17, 2026 | 4:45 PM – 5:45 PM (Class dismissal surge)
- **Observer:** Research Team Member (Silent Observation)

| Dimension | Real Observations Noted in Field |
|---|---|
| **A (Activities)** | Hundreds of students exiting through the turnstiles; a student attempting to hand over a found pair of earbuds; guard pointing to a side cardboard box without logging details. |
| **E (Environments)** | High noise, narrow turnstile choke point, rapid vehicle movements at the barrier. |
| **I (Interactions)** | Quick 15-second handovers between hurried finders and busy guards; no receipt or acknowledgment slip issued to the finder. |
| **O (Objects)** | Cardboard carton labeled "Lost Items" under the guard's wooden table; multiple identical black umbrellas, steel water bottles, and notebooks. |
| **U (Users)** | Gate security officers, rushing students, delivery riders entering the building. |

---

## Key Synthesis from Fieldwork:
1. **The Handover Black Hole:** Once an item is handed to a guard or desk, it enters an unindexed physical void.
2. **The Verification Dilemma:** Without structured metadata (brand, color, hidden distinguishing marks), neither the guard nor the library staff can safely release high-value items.
