# Field Research Pack: 8 User Interview Records
**Project ID:** P04 · Lost on a 600-Acre Campus  
**Confidentiality Compliance:** In accordance with Section 11 of the INT335 handbook, all participant names have been replaced with anonymous identifiers. Verbal consent was obtained prior to all sessions.

---

### Interview 1: Participant S1 (The Distressed Loser)
- **Role / Profile:** 2nd-year B.Tech CSE student (Hosteller, Block 14)
- **Location:** Central Library 2nd Floor Reading Room
- **Date & Time:** September 14, 2026 at 4:30 PM
- **Language:** English / Hindi
- **Context:** Lost a Casio FX-991EX scientific calculator two days before engineering mathematics examination.
- **Key Quotes:**
  - *"I lost my Casio calculator before Mid-Terms. I posted in 4 WhatsApp groups. Someone messaged me claiming it was theirs just to get a free calculator."*
  - *"I had engraved my initials lightly on the back battery lid, but because I posted a photo showing the front, anyone could claim they were the owner."*
- **Key Insights:** Open social media broadcasts enable opportunistic impostors to claim high-value academic tools without proving genuine ownership.

---

### Interview 2: Participant S2 (The Access-Locked Fresher)
- **Role / Profile:** 1st-year B.Tech student (Hosteller, Block 28)
- **Location:** Block 34 Help Desk lobby
- **Date & Time:** September 15, 2026 at 1:15 PM
- **Context:** Lost campus RFID smart ID card during orientation week.
- **Key Quotes:**
  - *"I lost my LPU ID card during freshers week. Without it, I couldn't enter the hostel mess or library for 4 days until re-issue."*
  - *"Every guard asked me for proof of identity, but my only identity card was the one I had lost."*
- **Key Insights:** Losing an ID card creates an immediate administrative deadlock, cutting off daily nutrition (mess) and academic resources.

---

### Interview 3: Participant S3 (The Hurried Finder)
- **Role / Profile:** 3rd-year Mechanical Engineering student (Day scholar)
- **Location:** UniMall Food Court staircase
- **Date & Time:** September 16, 2026 at 12:45 PM
- **Context:** Discovered a set of bike keys attached to a distinctive red lanyard.
- **Key Quotes:**
  - *"I found a set of bike keys with a red lanyard near the stairs. I had class in 5 minutes, so I just left them on the counter. I didn't know who to give them to."*
  - *"If there were a simple drop box or a 1-minute way to log it, I would have happily reported it."*
- **Key Insights:** Finders are not malicious; they face severe time constraints between classes and lack clear, immediate drop-off channels.

---

### Interview 4: Participant S4 (The Privacy-Vulnerable Student)
- **Role / Profile:** 2nd-year ECE student (Day scholar)
- **Location:** Block 36 Corridors
- **Date & Time:** September 18, 2026 at 2:30 PM
- **Context:** Friend lost a leather wallet containing cash, student card, and bus pass.
- **Key Quotes:**
  - *"When my friend lost her wallet, she posted her phone number on an Instagram confessions page. She got 15 creepy prank calls and never got the wallet back."*
  - *"People feel forced to give their personal WhatsApp numbers because there is no other platform."*
- **Key Insights:** Public lost-and-found channels expose female students and day scholars to spam, harassment, and privacy violations.

### Interview 5: Participant S5 (The Commuter Day Scholar)
- **Role / Profile:** 4th-year CSE student (Commutes from Jalandhar daily)
- **Location:** Two-Wheeler Parking Lot 2
- **Date & Time:** September 19, 2026 at 5:10 PM
- **Context:** Misplaced bus pass and laptop charger.
- **Key Quotes:**
  - *"I commute 25 km daily. Losing my bus pass on campus ruined my entire week of attendance."*
  - *"Security desks close or guards rotate shifts, so by the time I return the next morning, the guard on duty knows nothing about what was handed over yesterday."*
- **Key Insights:** Guard shift rotations create broken paper chains-of-custody, making physical recovery unpredictable across different days.

### Interview 6: Participant S6 (The Passive Resigner)
- **Role / Profile:** 1st-year MBA student (Hosteller)
- **Location:** Central Library Water Point
- **Date & Time:** September 20, 2026 at 11:20 AM
- **Context:** Lost an insulated steel water bottle.
- **Key Quotes:**
  - *"I left my blue Milton bottle on study desk 14. When I came back 30 minutes later, it was gone. Nobody turned it in."*
  - *"I didn't bother checking security because I assumed whoever took it just kept it."*
- **Key Insights:** Students suffer from low recovery expectation; they assume items are stolen rather than waiting in an unindexed security box.

### Interview 7: Participant G1 (Campus Security Officer)
- **Role / Profile:** Senior Security Guard (Officer Gurpreet)
- **Location:** Block 34 Main Entrance Security Post
- **Date & Time:** September 21, 2026 at 3:15 PM
- **Language:** Punjabi / Hindi
- **Context:** Manages gate traffic and accepts items turned in by students.
- **Key Quotes:**
  - *"We have a cardboard box full of water bottles, notebooks, and keys under the table. Students rarely come to ask because they assume it's stolen, not submitted."*
  - *"When a student comes asking for a lost black umbrella, I have 6 black umbrellas. Without proof, I cannot give it away."*
- **Key Insights:** Security personnel are overwhelmed by unindexed physical inventory and have no objective verification method to resolve identical claims.

---

### Interview 8: Participant G2 (Library Circulation Supervisor)
- **Role / Profile:** Library Help Desk Caretaker (Staff M.)
- **Location:** Central Library Circulation Counter
- **Date & Time:** September 22, 2026 at 10:45 AM
- **Context:** Handles lost personal items found after library closing hours.
- **Key Quotes:**
  - *"Every exam week, students bring 5 to 10 lost calculators and glasses. Matching them to the real owner is impossible because they all look identical."*
  - *"If a student could show me a verified digital receipt or match code on their phone, it would take me 5 seconds to hand over the item safely."*
- **Key Insights:** Institutional staff crave a lightweight digital verification handshake to eliminate dispute liability and protect student belongings.
