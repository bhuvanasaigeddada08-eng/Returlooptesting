# Ideation Pack: 32 Ideas Log & Selection Matrix

## 1. Ideation Methods & 32 Ideas Log

### Method A: Brainstorming (Ideas 1–12)
1. Digital campus lost-and-found board categorized by blocks.
2. QR code stickers distributed during orientation to stick onto calculators and laptops.
3. Cardboard drop boxes placed outside every academic block gate.
4. WhatsApp auto-responder bot keyword-matching lost reports.
5. Campus radio announcement during evening library closure.
6. Push notifications sent to students whose timetables match the room where an item was found.
7. RFID tags embedded into student ID smart cards with gate scanners.
8. Daily email digest sent by Division of Student Affairs with recovered items.
9. Drone-based delivery of verified lost items directly to hostel gates.
10. Community bounty / reward points system giving hostel canteen credits for returned items.
11. Lost-item kiosk booth in UniMall with touch screen search.
12. Security guard mobile tablet app replacing physical paper registers.

### Method B: Crazy 8s (Ideas 13–20)
13. *One-Click Report:* 30-second form taking photo, location, and category.
14. *Hidden Proof Vault:* Loser specifies a secret mark (e.g. scratch, sticker) that only the finder and security can see.
15. *Masked Contact Flow:* Loser and finder communicate via anonymous relay without exposing phone numbers.
16. *Physical-Digital Tag:* When guard puts item in box, they write a 4-digit code matching the online listing.
17. *Multi-Language Audio Assistant:* Voice prompts in Punjabi and Hindi for campus cleaning staff.
18. *Handover QR Pass:* Claimant shows an encrypted digital QR pass on their phone to the guard at the gate.
19. *Automated 5-Trait Match Engine:* Automatically links lost and found reports sharing Category, Location, Date, Brand, and Color.
20. *Offline Emergency Paper Slips:* Triplicate carbon-copy slips at security gates synced at shift end.

### Method C: SCAMPER (Ideas 21–32)
21. **Substitute:** Replace public phone numbers with secure digital ownership claim tickets.
22. **Substitute:** Replace manual keyword searching with an automated similarity scoring engine.
23. **Combine:** Combine online discovery with physical verification at campus security desks.
24. **Combine:** Combine lost-and-found tracking with student ID authentication.
25. **Adapt:** Adapt courier parcel tracking timelines (Reported $\rightarrow$ Verified $\rightarrow$ Ready for Handover $\rightarrow$ Claimed).
26. **Modify:** Blur high-value identifying marks on public photos to prevent photographic cloning.
27. **Modify:** Provide 7 regional Indian languages so all campus staff can navigate the interface.
28. **Put to another use:** Use unclaimed items after 90 days for campus charity book/stationery drives.
29. **Eliminate:** Eliminate open comments and chat threads to prevent spam, trolling, and harassment.
30. **Eliminate:** Eliminate external cloud server dependencies by running a ₹0 self-hosted local stack.
31. **Reverse:** Instead of the loser searching through hundreds of items, let the system push matches to them.
32. **Reverse:** Let finders hand over items to security first, then let security handle verified claims.
