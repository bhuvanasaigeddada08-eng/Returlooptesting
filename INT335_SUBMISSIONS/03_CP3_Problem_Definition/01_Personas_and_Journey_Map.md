# User Personas & Journey Map

## Persona 1: Aarav Sharma (The Distressed Loser)
- **Age:** 20 | **Course:** B.Tech CSE (2nd Year) | **Residence:** Boys Hostel Block 14
- **Quote:** *"I don't mind someone checking my item, but I hate that 200 random people in a WhatsApp group now have my personal phone number."*
- **Goals:**
  - Recover his HP laptop and Casio calculator before the Mid-Term exam.
  - Avoid spending ₹1,500 on duplicate registrations and replacement calculators.
  - Protect his personal contact details from pranksters and scammers.
- **Frustrations:**
  - WhatsApp groups get flooded with memes and chatter, burying his lost report.
  - Opportunistic strangers try to claim items that do not belong to them.
  - Security guards rotate shifts; morning guards know nothing about evening handovers.

## Persona 2: Riya Verma (The Busy Finder)
- **Age:** 21 | **Course:** B.Sc Biotechnology (3rd Year) | **Residence:** Day Scholar (Jalandhar)
- **Quote:** *"I want to do the right thing and return what I found, but I only have 10 minutes between lectures and cannot walk all the way across campus."*
- **Goals:**
  - Safely hand over found items (wallets, keys, headphones) to a verified authority.
  - Complete the handover in under 1 minute without missing her bus or lecture.
  - Avoid false accusations or being held liable for damaged property.
- **Frustrations:**
  - No clear campus protocol on where to hand over found belongings.
  - Unwilling to post on social media because she doesn't want strangers messaging her.
  - Security desks often lack formal receipts or confirmation.
