SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM: PHYSICAL CHAOS ACROSS A 600-ACRE CAMPUS
Subtitle: Campus Scale Bottlenecks & Failure of Existing Recovery Channels

### Campus Reality at LPU
• Vast Geographical Scale: 600 acres, 35,000+ students, 60+ academic blocks (Blocks 32–38), Central Library, UniMall, and 40 residential hostels.
• The 10-Minute Transition Rush: Students rushing between lectures leave belongings behind on study desks, lab benches, and cafeteria tables.
• High-Value Academic Stakes: Scientific calculators (₹1,500), RFID ID cards, laptops, and spectacle cases lost right before examinations.

### Current Failure Modes
• Unindexed Cardboard Boxes: Security desks accumulate items in cardboard boxes; Block 34 currently holds 6 unindexed black umbrellas with zero claim records.
• Chaotic Social Media Groups: Students post on unofficial WhatsApp and Instagram confession boards, leaking phone numbers to strangers.
• Opportunistic Fraud: Publicly visible photos invite false claims from impostors seeking free electronics.

Speaker Notes: Across 600 acres, losing an item triggers immense panic. Finders have only 10 minutes between lectures and don't know where to turn items in, while losers are forced to broadcast personal phone numbers on chaotic WhatsApp groups.

SLIDE 4: POINT OF VIEW (POV) & PRIORITIZED 'HOW MIGHT WE'S
Subtitle: Reframing Campus Friction into Actionable Design Challenges

### Our Point of View (POV) Statement
[USER] University students carrying essential academic belongings across a 600-acre campus...
[NEED] ...need an authenticated, privacy-preserving recovery channel without exposing their personal phone numbers...
[INSIGHT] ...because informal social media groups invite fraudulent claims, spam harassment, and permanent property loss.

### Prioritized How-Might-We (HMW) Questions
1. HMW #1 (Core Privacy): How might we verify genuine ownership instantly without exposing personal phone numbers or confidential student data to public boards?
2. HMW #2 (Finder Friction): How might we make reporting a found item take under 60 seconds so hurried students drop it off before their next class?
3. HMW #3 (Admin Governance): How might we empower campus security guards to verify and hand over items in regional Indian languages with zero paperwork?

Speaker Notes: We crystallized our research into our POV formula and 3 actionable HMW questions focusing on zero phone leaks, sub-60 second reporting, and multi-lingual guard enablement.

SLIDE 5: IDEATION & SELECTION: 32 IDEAS TRIAGE & SCAMPER
Subtitle: Divergent Brainstorming, Crazy 8s & Feasibility vs Impact Screening

### 32 Concepts Across 4 Clusters
• Cluster 1 (AI & Matching): 5-Trait Vector Matching, Photo Color Palette Match, WhatsApp Alert Bot, Heatmap Radar.
• Cluster 2 (Privacy & Proof): Masked Listings, Private Proof Vault, One-Time QR Claim Slip, UID Student Proxy.
• Cluster 3 (Physical Infrastructure): Smart IoT Lockers, Color-Coded Bins, Security Tablet Desk, Dormitory Return Box.
• Cluster 4 (Inclusion & Staff): 7 Indian Regional Languages, Guard Voice Input, Honesty Badges, Sub-60s Fast Flow.

### SCAMPER & 2x2 Selection Matrix
• Substitute: Replace public phone numbers with privacy-masked listings.
• Reverse: Items automatically search and find owners via trait matching.
• Screening Decision: High-Capex physical IoT smart lockers were deferred; ₹0-cost software solution with Secret Proof Verification and 7 Regional Languages won 1st rank.

Speaker Notes: Through SCAMPER and our 2x2 Feasibility vs Impact matrix, we rejected expensive smart lockers and prioritized a software-mediated secret verification engine that works on existing student smartphones at ₹0 cost.

SLIDE 6: SYSTEM INNOVATION: THE 5 PILLARS OF RETURNLOOP LPU
Subtitle: Core Differentiators That Guarantee Privacy, Speed & Zero Fraud

### 5 Core Innovations
1. 5-Trait Similarity Matching: Background algorithm matches category, brand, model, color, and location in &lt; 20ms.
2. Privacy-Masked Listings: Public items conceal personal names and phone numbers; 100% protection against spam.
3. Secret Identifier Verification: Hidden serial numbers, lock screens, and engravings are visible ONLY to campus security.

### Campus-Wide Inclusivity
4. 7 Indian Regional Languages: Complete UI localization in Hindi, Punjabi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, and English.
5. Campus Security Admin Desk: Dedicated portal enabling gate guards to inspect proof, verify student ID cards, and record an immutable audit trail.

Speaker Notes: ReturnLoop is built on 5 pillars: 5-trait similarity matching, privacy-masked public listings, secret identifier verification, 7 regional Indian languages, and a digital security admin desk.

SLIDE 7: PROTOTYPE & USER FLOW: PAPER MOCKUPS TO LIVE PWA
Subtitle: From Low-Fidelity Paper Sketches to Functional Web Deployment

### Storyboard & Architecture
• 6-Panel Storyboard: Loss Event in Lab ➔ Instant 42s Mobile Report ➔ Trait Auto-Match ➔ Alert Notification ➔ Security Desk Verification ➔ Safe Handover.
• Tech Stack: React 18 + Vite + Tailwind CSS + Python Flask API.
• Live Deployment URL: https://returlooptesting-khwd.vercel.app/

### Embedded User Flow & Screens
• Loser Path: Selects category, enters model, inputs secret proof (RV initials on battery cover).
• Finder Path: Snaps photo at UniMall stairs, selects drop location, submits in &lt; 45s.
• Security Path: Compares physical item with owner's encrypted digital proof.

Speaker Notes: We tested on paper first, then developed the responsive web app deployed live on Vercel. Finders report in 45 seconds, and owners verify claims with security guards.

SLIDE 8: USABILITY TESTING & 4 CORE ENGINEERING FIXES
Subtitle: Empirical Verification Across 6 Campus Users & Technical Iterations

### Quantitative Testing Results
• 6 Participants Tested: Rohan Verma, Aman Sharma, Simranjeet Kaur, Sahil Gupta, Officer Gurpreet Singh, Staff Manjit Kumar.
• 100% Task Completion Rate: All participants successfully reported, searched, or verified claims.
• Average Task Time: Reduced to 38 seconds.

### The 4 Core Engineering Fixes
1. Mobile WebKit Clipping Fix: React <code>createPortal</code> solves iPhone safe-area modal cutoff.
2. Staff Language Barrier Fix: Added Punjabi/Hindi UI so guards verify items in 15s.
3. Secret Proof Tooltip Fix: Visual lock icon clarifies private proof vs public text.
4. Vite Build Fix: Unified <code>TranslatedText</code> component resolved import error.

Speaker Notes: We tested with 6 users. Testing revealed that security guards needed Punjabi/Hindi and iPhone users experienced modal clipping. We implemented 4 critical engineering fixes in code.
