### The Team's POV Statement & Why This Concept Was Selected
- **POV Statement:** *University students carrying essential academic belongings across a 600-acre campus need an authenticated, privacy-preserving recovery channel without exposing their personal phone numbers because informal social media groups invite fraudulent claims, spam harassment, and permanent property loss.*
- **Why this concept was selected:** It solves the root cause of lost-item friction—fear of fraud and loss of privacy—at **₹0 external cost**, without requiring expensive hardware installations or exposing student phone numbers to the public.
