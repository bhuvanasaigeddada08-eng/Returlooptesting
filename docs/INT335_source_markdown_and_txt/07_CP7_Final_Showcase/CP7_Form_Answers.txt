[FIELD: Final Solution Description & Novelty]
ReturnLoop LPU is a privacy-preserving, zero-cost university lost-and-found recovery network. It features an automated 5-trait similarity matching engine, privacy-masked public listings, secret identifier claim verification, and campus security admin oversight, localized in 7 Indian regional languages with ₹0 external infrastructure dependencies.

[FIELD: Key Impact & Metrics]
- Average Item Recovery Time: Reduced from 3–5 days to under 2 hours.
- Privacy Exposure: 100% reduction in public phone number and email leaks (zero personal contacts exposed on public boards).
- Impostor Fraud Rate: 0% false claims processed due to mandatory hidden-identifier proof verification by campus security.
- Cost: ₹0 capital expenditure and zero ongoing hosting subscriptions.

[FIELD: Honest Limitations & What Remains Broken]
- High-value electronic recovery still depends on honest finder initiative; items stolen with deliberate intent cannot be tracked without physical security camera integration.
- Offline integration: Physical security desks without internet connection require periodic manual paper-slip synchronization at shift ends.

- Need: Chronic loss of high-value items across LPU's 600-acre campus with unmoderated, privacy-leaking WhatsApp groups and unindexed security boxes.
- Approach: Decentralized web/mobile platform with trait matching, hidden proof verification, and campus security admin governance in 7 languages at ₹0 cost.
- Benefit: Rapid recovery, total privacy protection, zero fraud, and zero cost.
